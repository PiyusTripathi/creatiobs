VIDEO EDITING GUIDE

Video editing combines storytelling, rhythm, audio, color and technical export knowledge. A useful course should train students through practical edits, not only theory.

Software to learn

Premiere Pro is commonly used for editing, while After Effects helps with titles, motion graphics and animation. Photoshop is useful for thumbnails and supporting design assets.

Portfolio projects

Reels, promotional edits, YouTube-style videos, title animations and event cuts help beginners show their editing range.
