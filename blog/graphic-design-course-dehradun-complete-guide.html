GRAPHIC DESIGN GUIDE

A good graphic design course should teach more than software buttons. It should help you understand layout, color, typography, branding, image editing, social media design, print output and portfolio presentation.

What should the syllabus include?

Look for design fundamentals first, then Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, branding, social media creatives, poster design, brochures and portfolio projects. The best learning happens when every tool is connected to a real brief.

Why projects matter

Projects show whether a student can apply the learning. Brand identities, campaign sets and print layouts give admissions teams, clients and employers something real to evaluate.
