How to Choose a Graphic Design Institute in Dehradun

By Creatiobs Team / Published Oct 14, 2026 / Updated Oct 14, 2026

INSTITUTE CHECKLIST

Choose an institute by looking at the work students create, not only by reading a course name. A strong institute should show practical training, mentor guidance and portfolio outcomes.

Checklist

Review the syllabus, software, project quality, batch support, student feedback, career guidance and whether the institute helps you build a portfolio.

Ask before admission

Ask about duration, fees, tools, assignments, review process and whether genuine student projects are available to inspect.
