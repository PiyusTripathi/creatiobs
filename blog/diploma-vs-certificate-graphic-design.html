Diploma vs Certificate in Graphic Design

By Creatiobs Team / Published Oct 14, 2026 / Updated Oct 14, 2026

DIPLOMA VS CERTIFICATE

A certificate is usually shorter and focused on a defined skill track. A diploma is broader and gives more time for multiple tools, project depth and career preparation.

Which should you choose?

Choose a certificate if you want focused skill development. Choose a diploma if you want broader exposure across graphic design, video editing, motion graphics and UI/UX.
