How to Become a Graphic Designer

By Creatiobs Team / Published Oct 14, 2026 / Updated Oct 14, 2026

DESIGN CAREER

Start with fundamentals: layout, color, typography and visual hierarchy. Then learn tools like Photoshop and Illustrator through practical assignments.

Build proof

A portfolio is the proof of your skill. Create brand identity, social media, poster, print and campaign projects that show range.
