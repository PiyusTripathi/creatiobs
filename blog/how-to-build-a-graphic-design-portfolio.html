How to Build a Graphic Design Portfolio

By Creatiobs Team / Published Oct 14, 2026 / Updated Oct 14, 2026

PORTFOLIO

A beginner portfolio should show clear thinking and finished output. Include fewer strong projects instead of many incomplete experiments.

Projects to include

Brand identity, social media campaign, poster, brochure, packaging concept, UI screen and a before-after redesign are useful starting points.

Portfolio development is part of the Graphic Design Course and the Diploma Program.
