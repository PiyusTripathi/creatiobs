FAQ

UI/UX Design Questions

Is coding required for UI/UX design?

No. This course focuses on design thinking, visual interfaces, Figma, prototypes and portfolio presentation.

Will I use Figma?

Yes. Figma is the primary UI/UX design tool in this learning path.

Can graphic design students learn UI/UX?

Yes. Graphic design fundamentals are helpful, and this course builds digital product skills on top of visual design thinking.
